---
description: Warehousing & Inventory Management Workflow
---

This workflow documents the Warehousing operations as shown in the sidebar navigation.

## 1. Overview
**Goal**: High-level view of warehouse capacity and movement.
- Navigate to: **Warehousing > Overview** (`/warehousing`)
- Action: Review "Total Utilization", "Inbound Expected", and "Outbound Pending".

## 2. Inventory Management
**Goal**: Manage stock levels and bin locations.
- Navigate to: **Warehousing > Inventory** (`/warehousing/inventory`)
- Action: Search for specific SKUs or Containers.
- Action: Update stock counts or move items between bins.

## 3. Gate Passes (In/Out)
**Goal**: Control the physical movement of goods and vehicles.
- Navigate to: **Warehousing > Gate Passes** (`/warehousing`)
- Action: Create **Inward Gate Pass** for arriving goods.
- Action: Create **Outward Gate Pass** for dispatched orders.
- Validation: Ensure vehicle number and driver CNIC are recorded.

## 4. Reports
**Goal**: Generate stock and movement reports.
- Navigate to: **Warehousing > Reports** (`/warehousing/reports`)
- Action: Generate "Stock Ageing Report", "Movement History", or "Utilization Report".
